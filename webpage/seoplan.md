# Fix rendering first, then own Hindi crusher search

CrusherBook's biggest SEO problem is not missing keywords. Google cannot properly see the pages it already has. Every URL on crusherbook.com returns the same empty 4,994-byte HTML shell, and that shell's canonical tag points to the homepage. Unknown URLs return 200, www and non-www both serve the site, the live sitemap is out of date, and the structured data claims a 4.8-star rating from 250 reviews that cannot be verified. Week 1-2 should go entirely to prerendering every route and removing false or unsupported claims ([Google JS SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics); [Google review snippet rules](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)). Once that is fixed, the opportunity is large. The English software keywords ("stone crusher software", "...billing", "...accounting", "...ERP", "...free download") are low in volume but weakly contested by small vendors and marketplaces. Hindi and Hinglish searches around crusher accounting have almost no software content competing for them. The real traffic sits in neighbouring topics that owners and munshis search every day: kanta parchi and weighbridge slip formats, GST/HSN for gitti, brass/CFT/ton conversions, crusher profit and setup cost, truck and hyva rent, and the royalty-portal how-tos. CrusherBook can win these with free tools, templates, bilingual guides and Hindi YouTube. It also has a clear positioning advantage: every crusher-specific competitor with a public price charges more than CrusherBook's Rs 6,999-10,999 a year, and the enterprise players (SAZS, Inniti, Quarry360) hide their prices. One caveat applies to everything below. No paid keyword tool was available, so every volume figure is a relative estimate based on Google autocomplete. Validate the shortlist in Keyword Planner and Search Console before investing heavily in any one cluster.

## The site is invisible because crawlers get an empty shell with a homepage canonical

A direct check on 2026-10-11 found that `/`, `/crusher-billing-software`, `/features/` and `/nonexistent-page-xyz` all return **HTTP 200 with the identical index.html**. That file contains only `<div id="root"></div>`, the homepage title, and `<link rel="canonical" href="https://crusherbook.com/">`. `Seo.jsx` (react-helmet-async) then rewrites the canonical in JavaScript. Google says the best way to set a canonical is in HTML, that you "shouldn't use JavaScript to change the canonical URL to something else than the URL you specified", and that server-side or pre-rendering "is still a great idea" ([Google JS SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics)). So all ten landing pages tell Google, in their raw HTML, that they are copies of the homepage. That is the most likely reason the site did not surface for `site:crusherbook.com` or its own brand query, while its **Capterra India listing ranked first for "stone crusher management software India"** ([Capterra IN](https://www.capterra.in/software/1241879/crusherbook)). The search tool used was not Google, so confirm actual index status in Search Console before and after the fix.

The audit found more problems. `www.crusherbook.com` serves 200 with no redirect, and `/features/` does not redirect to `/features`. The **492 KB JavaScript bundle is served uncompressed and without Cache-Control**, which matters for rural mobile users who see nothing until it loads. The live sitemap lists 11 URLs while the repo lists 15, and the live bundle hash differs from the local build, so the four newest keyword pages are probably not deployed. There is no 404 route, so every mistyped URL is a soft 404. WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X do not run JavaScript, so every shared CrusherBook link previews as the homepage. For a WhatsApp-driven business that also hurts word of mouth ([dev.to SPA SEO](https://dev.to/ali_dz/optimizing-seo-in-a-react-vite-project-the-ultimate-guide-3mbh)).

The trust problems are just as urgent because they put the site at risk of a penalty. `index.html` and `Home.jsx` both emit `aggregateRating` 4.8 from 250 ratings, but no reviews are visible on the page and Capterra shows zero. Google requires ratings to be "sourced directly from users" and visible on the page ([Google review snippet rules](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)). `Features.jsx` claims "full offline support" and an "Offline Variant + Lifetime Plan". The product has neither, and the lifetime plan contradicts the annual pricing. The Features title also advertises "Royalty", and the quarry page copy mentions royalty, which the app does not support. Smaller problems: titles render as "... | CrusherBook | CrusherBook". The Organization schema uses the invalid property `contact`, lists `support@crusherbook.com` while the footer shows `softwarebytesindia@gmail.com`, and appears twice on each page. The Hindi page declares `lang="en"`. The OG image is misspelled `cruhserbook.webp`. The six named homepage testimonials could not be verified as real customers.

**Prerendering is the fix that everything else depends on.** The lowest-effort route for this Vite + React 19 + `BrowserRouter` stack is a post-build headless-browser snapshot (Puppeteer or Playwright) that writes `dist/<slug>/index.html` for every route in `landingPages.js`. Each file should carry its own title, description, canonical, OG tags and body HTML. nginx then needs `try_files $uri $uri/index.html =404` so unknown paths return a real 404. Two heavier alternatives are React Router framework mode with `prerender()` ([React Router rendering docs](https://reactrouter.com/start/framework/rendering)) and `vite-react-ssg`, whose 2026 maintenance status was not checked.

## Keyword targets: win every small software variant, then capture the large adjacent searches

Google autocomplete for India shows the software cluster is real but small. "stone crusher software" completes to free download, billing, management, accounting, best, ERP and plant management ([autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=stone+crusher+software)). All software variants together probably total only low hundreds of searches a month. That is still worth having, because each one comes from a likely buyer. Much larger demand sits around the owner's daily work: weighbridge slip formats ([autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=weighbridge+slip)), gitti GST rate and HSN ([autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=gitti+gst)), CFT/brass to ton ([autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=cft+to+ton)), stone crusher profit per day/month/ton ([autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=stone+crusher+profit)) and hyva/tipper rent ([autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=hyva+rent)).

In Hindi autocomplete, exact crusher-accounting phrases ("crusher ka hisab", "crusher munshi", "crusher udhari", "dumper ka hisab") return **no completions at all**, on Google or YouTube. Neighbouring terms do have clear demand: "kanta parchi kaise nikale", "truck/tractor/jcb ka hisab", "gitti ka rate", "royalty kaise nikale" and portal names such as "khanij online cg" ([Hindi autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=kanta+parchi)). So do not build new pages for exact phrases nobody types. Keep them as secondary terms on the existing Hinglish page, and put real effort into the neighbouring topics.

Volume scale used in the tables (relative, not measured): **High** means many geographic or unit variants, likely thousands a month. **Medium** means several variants, likely hundreds. **Low** means it autocompletes with few variants, likely tens to low hundreds. **Very low** means no autocomplete. ## Seven keyword overlaps need fixing before any new page goes live

The ten landing pages all sit in one narrow "crusher/quarry software" cluster, and several declare the same keywords in `landingPages.js`. Google's spam policy describes "substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy" as doorway pages ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). Adding more synonym pages raises that risk. Fix the overlaps first, then grow only into genuinely different intents.

| Overlap | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| /stone-crusher-management-app and /crusher-hisab-kitab-app both list "stone crusher app" | Two pages compete for one term | Keep "stone crusher app" on the app page only. Make the hisab page purely Hindi/Hinglish (crusher ka hisab, udhari, party hisab), set `lang="hi"` (or `hi-Latn`), and use Devanagari H1/H2 |
| /quarry-management-software vs /quarry-management-system | Google treats "software" and "system" as the same intent | Preferred: 301 /quarry-management-system to /quarry-management-software. Alternative: rewrite "system" around multi-user roles and permissions and drop "quarry ERP system" and "quarry accounting system" |
| "quarry and crusher ERP" (quarry-management-software) and "quarry ERP system India" (quarry-management-system) | Both would collide with a new ERP page | Remove ERP terms from both quarry pages before launching /stone-crusher-erp-software |
| /stone-crusher-management-software vs /quarry-crusher-management-software ("crusher management system" vs "crusher plant management system") | Same head-term family | Stone-crusher page owns "crusher management system/software". ## The action plan: foundations in two weeks, tools and Hindi video by month six

The order matters. New content placed on a site whose raw HTML says every page is the homepage will not rank. Reviews and listings build trust that the content later depends on. Tools and Hindi video then bring in the traffic, because those are the formats where competition is weakest.

## Conclusion

The data shows CrusherBook is fighting the wrong battle if it adds more synonym landing pages. Its competitors are weak on transparency, Hindi and trust, but the site has been weaker still on basic crawlability. Ten pages whose raw HTML points to the homepage are effectively one page. Most of the potential gain in the next 90 days comes from fixes that take a developer a few days: prerendering, the canonical, the 404 handling, the redirect and the removal of unsupported claims. The growth after that will not come mainly from "stone crusher software" variants, which are too small to carry the business. It will come from being the most useful Hindi-friendly resource on the arithmetic crusher owners and munshis do every day: slips, conversions, GST, profit and truck rent. Each tool and video in that set leads naturally into the product.

The biggest open question is how large these clusters really are. Every volume label here is inferred from autocomplete, not measured. The most valuable early investment after the technical fixes is therefore measurement: a Keyword Planner pass on this shortlist with India and Hindi settings, and 8-12 weeks of Search Console impressions on the first new pages. Once real numbers exist, shift effort away from clusters that show no impressions and toward the ones that do, rather than treating this plan as fixed.
