# CrusherBook competitor landscape and off-site reach opportunities (India, October 2026)

Research date: 16 Oct 2026. About 20 searches and fetches were run. No paid SEO tool (Ahrefs or Semrush backlinks, Similarweb traffic) was available, and Capterra, chrome-stats and the Play Store either blocked or truncated the fetches. So this file has **no measured backlink or traffic figures** for any competitor, and every "ranks for" statement is inferred from which pages appeared in search results. Earlier baseline: the competitor table in `webpage/seoplan.md`, referred to below as "the earlier table".

## Which companies compete with CrusherBook, and what are their pricing, SEO/content assets and weaknesses?

### Takeaway
The niche is still fragmented. No player has public reviews or Hindi web content at scale. There are three tiers. (1) Enterprise and automation ERPs that hide their prices: SAZS, Inniti, Quarry360/Syvasoft and Quipu. These are the most active SEO and event marketers, and SAZS and Quipu have added fresh 2026 content. (2) Cheap billing tools on marketplaces: LogiBit "StoneCrusher Software" on Techjockey, Vyapar, and IndiaMART desktop vendors. (3) Crusher Suvidha, a Hindi/Hinglish marketplace-plus-tools app that is the only Hindi-native rival. CrusherBook's own marketplace listings are the weakest link: the Techjockey listing still shows old prices and the Capterra listing has 0 reviews.

### Inferences
- **Price positioning is still unique.** Among crusher-specific products with a public price, CrusherBook's Rs 6,999-10,999/year sits below LogiBit (Rs 13,555-22,555) and far below the IndiaMART vendors (Rs 80,000/yr; Rs 45,000/month). SAZS, Inniti, Quarry360 and Quipu all hide prices. A sourced "stone crusher software price in India" comparison page remains a strong, low-competition asset. It must be dated and must cite each listing.
- **Quipu's "10 things to check before you buy" (Sept 2026) competes directly** with CrusherBook's new beginner's buying guide (commit 277d822). Quipu is now the closest content-SEO competitor for buyer-intent informational queries.
- **SAZS's real moat is trade shows plus case studies.** It shows up at EXCON, IEMS, CODDISSIA and the Jharkhand show. Its weakness remains South India, English only and no price. The Hindi belt (CG, MP, UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, Rajasthan) looks under-served by every enterprise vendor's content.
- **Crusher Suvidha is a possible partner as well as a competitor.** It is a marketplace and community app, not a full ledger/ERP. A listing or advertising on it could reach Hindi-speaking owners. Its exact traction is unknown.
- **Automation vendors (SAZS, Quarry360, Watsoo, Visuotech) own the ANPR, boom-barrier and FasTag keywords.** CrusherBook should not contest these and should position as the phone-first, simple option for small and mid plants.
- **Fixing the Techjockey listing is the single fastest win.** It is wrong on price, wrong on category and has no reviews, and Techjockey "vs" pages rank for crusher-software queries.

## Which software directories and marketplaces matter, and how do you get listed and reviewed?

### Takeaway
Techjockey and Capterra already have CrusherBook listed but are under-used. SoftwareSuggest offers a free listing but needs a company-domain email. IndiaMART is where crusher-software sellers actually list prices. Reviews are the scarce asset everywhere: LogiBit has 0 written reviews and CrusherBook has 0, so even 5-10 genuine reviews would lead the niche.

### Cited Findings
- **Techjockey** lists plans with "Get Quote" and builds automatic "X vs Y" and "alternatives" pages, which rank for crusher-software queries (for example "stonecrusher software vs apna billbook" and "vs billoro"). — [Techjockey compare](https://www.techjockey.com/compare/stonecrusher-software-vs-apna-billbook); [Techjockey CrusherBook](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/crusherbook)
- **SoftwareSuggest** has a free "List Your Product" option for vendors that does not accept Gmail, Outlook or Hotmail addresses, so a company-domain email is needed. Paid tiers reportedly run from about $4,000 per 6 months to $15,000 per 12 months, with PPC and MQL credits. Reviews are labelled invoice-verified or non-verified. Trustpilot feedback cited by a third party criticises lead quality. — [SoftwareSuggest vendors](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/vendors); [rfp.wiki](https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/softwaresuggest)
- **Capterra** lists vendors without payment and moderates reviews by hand (from the earlier plan). — [Capterra contact](https://www.capterra.in/company/contact). CrusherBook is already listed on capterra.in and capterra.co.uk with 0 ratings. — [Capterra IN](https://www.capterra.in/software/1241879/crusherbook)
- **IndiaMART** has a weighbridge-software category and crusher-software seller listings with visible prices. — [IndiaMART weighbridge category](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/weighbridge-software.html); [Automechano](https://m.indiamart.com/automechano-technologies/software.html)
- **Google Business Profile** requires a staffed location that customers can visit, rules out virtual offices, and does not allow keyword-stuffed names (from the earlier plan). — [GBP guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)

### Inferences
- **Priority order:** (1) Fix Techjockey prices and category, and collect reviews there, because its compare pages surface in search. (2) Get Capterra reviews; Gartner Digital Markets syndicates them to GetApp and Software Advice, which AI answer engines often cite. (3) List on SoftwareSuggest with a crusherbook.com or softwarebytes.in email. (4) List on IndiaMART with the price shown, because buyers there compare on price and CrusherBook's price beats theirs. (5) Add JustDial and GBP if the Raipur office qualifies. (6) Add G2, GoodFirms and SaaSworthy as lower-priority citation sources.
- The Play Store listing, if CrusherBook has an Android app, is a discovery and review channel. Crusher Suvidha and SAZS both use app stores.

### Gaps
- No direct data was gathered on how Techjockey, G2, GoodFirms or SaaSworthy rank vendors (paid placement versus reviews), or on JustDial listing terms.
- Not checked whether CrusherBook already has a Play Store listing, a JustDial listing or a GBP.

## Where do Indian crusher owners gather and learn (associations, media, trade shows, OEMs, social)?

### Takeaway
Crusher owners organise in **district and state associations** that make the news mostly over regulation (royalty, closures, new Acts). No national crusher-owner federation was found. The main trade shows are EXCON (Bengaluru, every two years, next Dec 2027), bauma CONEXPO India (Greater Noida, every two years) and regional shows (Jharkhand Mining & Construction Show, CODDISSIA Stone Quarry Expo, IEMS, TNMSA meets) where SAZS exhibits. I could not source the Facebook groups, WhatsApp groups or Hindi YouTube landscape.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Associations meet and lobby around regulation (royalty portals, new state Acts, closures). The way into them is plain-language Hindi explainers of new rules (for example Punjab's 2025 Act or royalty changes) plus member discounts. The realistic payoffs are a WhatsApp-group share, a speaking slot at a meeting and a link from any association website.
- SAZS's event list is a ready map of where crusher buyers gather. Regional shows near the Hindi belt (Jharkhand Mining & Construction Show; any CG/MP mining expos) are cheaper and better targeted for CrusherBook than EXCON or bauma.
- Crusher machine OEMs and dealers, weighbridge installers (Visuotech in Indore is close to the CG/MP market) and CAs serving crushers are the natural referral partners. No partnership programmes were found to confirm.

## Which link-building, PR, video and referral tactics are realistic for a small Indian SaaS?

### Takeaway
The highest-value moves are cheap and owned: fix and populate marketplace listings, earn reviews, publish named customer case studies (SAZS uses dated case studies as its main proof), build free calculators (SAZS's profit calculator and Quipu's leakage calculator show the format works in this niche), and win Hindi YouTube and WhatsApp, which no competitor occupies. PR and guest posts are secondary.

### Cited Findings
- Competitors use calculators as link and lead magnets: SAZS has a Quarry/Crusher/RMC profit calculator and Quipu has a "leakage calculator" with illustrative Rs 14.97 lakh/year figures. — [SAZS](https://sazsapps.com/); [Quipu](https://quipu.in/stone-crusher-software/)
- Case studies are SAZS's main content format (9 dated, by topic: theft, e-invoice, trips, profit, e-way bill). — [SAZS](https://sazsapps.com/)
- Vendors publish "Top 10 X companies in India 2026" listicles that rank and get cited (Watsoo for weighbridges). — [Watsoo](https://watsoo.com/blog/top-weighbridge-automation-companies-in-india/)
- WhatsApp delivery of weighment slips is already being marketed to dharam-kanta operators. — [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/whatsapp-weighbridge-dharam-kanta-operators-india-2026)
- Every competitor runs a demo-first sales route: SAZS offers a 30-minute remote demo, Quipu a demo on your own data with contact within one business day, and Inniti a demo or brochure. — [SAZS](https://sazsapps.com/); [Quipu](https://quipu.in/stone-crusher-software/); [Inniti](https://www.innitisoftware.com/stone-crusher-management-erp-system)
- Plant economics content attracts searchers (IMARC's 2026 setup-cost guide puts a 50-100 TPH plant at about Rs 1.5-4 crore and claims 25-35% net margins, which are vendor-style figures). — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/insight/stone-crushing-manufacturing-plant-setup-cost-india)

### Inferences (prioritised action list)
1. **Weeks 1-2: listings.** Correct the Techjockey prices (Rs 6,999 / 8,999 / 10,999), category and language (add Hindi). Create SoftwareSuggest (company email), IndiaMART (price shown) and JustDial listings. Use the same name, address, phone and URL (NAP) everywhere.
2. **Weeks 1-8: reviews.** Ask every active customer, by WhatsApp with a direct link, for a Capterra or Techjockey review and a Google review. The target is 10+, which would put CrusherBook ahead of every crusher competitor found (all have 0-4).
3. **Months 1-3: case studies.** Publish 3-4 named Chhattisgarh/MP plant stories (slips per day, udhari recovered, time saved), each with a short Hindi video. This copies SAZS's proof format in the Hindi belt it does not cover.
4. **Months 1-6: Hindi YouTube and Shorts.** Videos: "kanta parchi mobile se kaise banaye", "crusher ka roz ka hisab", "brass/CFT to ton", "truck/hyva bhada hisab", "crusher profit per ton", a 2-minute demo, and customer testimonials. Embed each on its matching page. No competitor's Hindi video presence was found, though this is unverified (see gaps).
5. **Months 2-6: free tools for links.** A CFT/brass-to-ton converter, a truck-load calculator, a crusher profit calculator and slip/ledger templates. Answer Quipu's "leakage calculator" with an honest, editable one.
6. **Ongoing: partnerships and referrals.** Approach weighbridge installers and software vendors (Visuotech, local dharam-kanta installers in CG/MP) and crusher machine dealers with a referral fee or a bundled offer. Approach state associations with rule explainers and member pricing. Approach Crusher Suvidha about a listing or advertising. Run a WhatsApp-first customer referral (a free month per referred plant) and a "Sent via CrusherBook" footer on shared slips and bills.
7. **Opportunistic PR.** Local Raipur/Chhattisgarh business press (a "Raipur startup digitises crushers" story), an AggBusiness pitch with anonymised Indian crusher data, and a booth or visit at regional mining shows (Jharkhand, CG) rather than EXCON at first.
8. **Comparison content.** Factual "CrusherBook vs Tally/Vyapar/Excel" pages and a sourced Indian crusher-software price comparison page. Answer Quipu's "10 things to check" with CrusherBook's own checklist guide, which is already published.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found on HARO-style Indian PR platforms or Raipur business press outlets that accept startup stories.
- No data on referral programmes run by any crusher-software competitor.
- The view counts and formats of crusher-related Hindi YouTube content need manual YouTube research.
