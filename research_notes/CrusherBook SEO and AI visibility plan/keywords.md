# CrusherBook keyword expansion: what crusher owners, munshis, weighbridge operators and buyers type (English, Hindi, Hinglish), October 2026

Method and evidence base (applies to every section below):
- On 2026-10-16 about 240 seed queries were run through four autocomplete endpoints: Google India English (`suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=`), Google India Hindi (`...&hl=hi`), YouTube (`...&ds=yt&hl=hi`) and Bing India (`api.bing.com/osjson.aspx?market=en-IN&query=`). Each returns at most 10 suggestions (Bing returns 12). YouTube result pages were scraped for view counts on 26 queries. Google SERPs, People Also Ask and Related searches could **not** be observed directly: the WebSearch tool is US-indexed, and google.co.in pages were not fetched. Competition was therefore judged from YouTube results, from the general web results that did come back, and from the two earlier reports.
- **No paid keyword tool was used, so every volume is a relative estimate.** Scale used: **High** = the seed fills all 10 Google slots with India-specific modifiers (cities, states, units, years, "today") and also shows up on YouTube and/or Bing. **Med** = 5-10 relevant Google suggestions, or strong YouTube views (>1 lakh on the top videos). **Low** = 1-4 suggestions. **None** = empty autocomplete. These labels are an ordering, not monthly search numbers.
- Citations take the form [AC-en], [AC-hi], [YT-AC] and [Bing], linking to the exact autocomplete URL, or [YT] for a YouTube results page. Readers can open any link to reproduce the result.
- Two earlier reports already cover these clusters: crusher/quarry software variants, kanta parchi app/kaise nikale/format, truck/gaadi/diesel/gitti ka hisab, truck bhada, crusher profit/business/kaise lagaye/kharcha, gitti ka rate, gitti GST/HSN in general, the brass/CFT/ton converter in general, the royalty portals in general, Tally vs app, gitti ka business, and the regional words (rodi/bajri/kapchi/khadi/jalli). Those reports are `webpage/seoplan.md` and `reports/Hindi crusher software keywords.md`. This file adds only **new** keywords, new evidence, or a change in verdict.

## 1. What people type around each topic (autocomplete, YouTube and Bing evidence)

### Takeaway
The biggest new demand is not "software" at all. It is **vehicle-capacity and unit questions**: how many cft/ton/brass fit in a hyva, 12-wheeler, dumper or tractor trolley, and how to measure gitti in a truck. Next come **road-material product queries** (GSB, WMM, crusher dust, M-sand, 20/40 mm aggregate: rate per ton/cft/brass/m3, density, HSN/GST), **machine hire** (JCB/poclain/dumper/tipper/hyva rent per hour/day/month, hire-charge HSN/SAC/TDS, rent agreement format), **JCB/poclain hour-meter and diesel-per-hour maths**, and **state mining-challan "check" queries**. All of these fill Google's 10 slots with India-specific modifiers. Hindi YouTube videos on measuring gitti and checking hyva capacity reach 10-26 lakh views, and no crusher-software vendor owns any of these topics.

### Inferences
- The search behaviour sorts into four personas, each with its own vocabulary. The **owner/investor** types English plus Hinglish (project report, profit, TPH, subsidy, loan, license). The **munshi/accountant** types English template words ("format in excel with formula", "register format", "quotation format", "party ledger"). The **weighbridge/vehicle operator** types Hinglish how-tos on YouTube (parchi kaise nikale, JCB reading, diesel average kaise nikale). The **buyer/contractor** types units and prices (hyva capacity, gitti kaise nape, gsb rate per ton, 1 truck gitti price). CrusherBook's sales pages speak only to the owner. The other three groups are the large audiences no crusher vendor has reached.
- "Format in excel **with formula**" shows up in diesel register, stock register, salary register, weighbridge slip, attendance register, quotation and vehicle log book. That is a consistent munshi signal: people want a working sheet, not a PDF. A free downloadable Excel/Google-Sheet template whose "next step" is CrusherBook is the most link- and download-friendly asset type for this audience.
- The new "2025/2026/new" modifiers on GST queries come from GST 2.0. Pages need a visible "last checked" date and a primary-source (CBIC) citation to win both Google and AI-assistant citations.
- Vehicle-capacity queries are the strongest link between buyer search and CrusherBook's per-ton vs per-cft billing. "Hyva me kitna cft / ton" is exactly the dispute that weighing on the kanta and billing by ton (or recording both, a feature in commit 8e1ea36) resolves.

### Gaps
- Real monthly volumes: no Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush data. Google Trends India was not queried.
- Google People Also Ask and Related searches for google.co.in were not observed, because the search tool is US-indexed. PAA claims in this file come only from autocomplete question forms.
- The current GST rate for HSN 2517/2505 after GST 2.0 was not confirmed from a CBIC notification.
- Whether CrusherBook computes vehicle average (km/l, l/hr) is unconfirmed. The brief says yes; the site copy and a code grep say no.

### Inferences
- **Suggested small-team sequence** (about 2 pieces/week):
  1. **Weeks 1-6, tools first** (#1, #12, #17 and the earlier converter #3), because tools win links and can be reused in videos. Then the material guides (#5/#6 merged, #7) and the HSN table (#10), which are evergreen and AI-quotable.

  3. **Months 4-6**: state hubs (#25) only for states where a customer story or local content exists. Then #26, #27, #28, #29, #13/#14, #23, #30.
- **URL conventions**: `/tools/…` for calculators, `/templates/…` for downloads, bare slugs for blogs. Hinglish Roman slugs for Hindi pages (consistent with the earlier report). One page per intent in both scripts.
- **Feature honesty**: #13/#14 must not claim automatic km/l or l/hr until it is confirmed shipped (see the conflict in section 1F). #24/#26/#27 must carry an "information only" disclaimer, since CrusherBook does not do royalty, TP or e-way bill.

Build one hire hub and make the other two sections of it. #23 must link to, not duplicate, `/crusher-udhari-payment-reminder-app` and `/crusher-plant-payment-reminder-software`. #19 must link to, not duplicate, `/crusher-plant-diesel-consumption-tracking-software`.
