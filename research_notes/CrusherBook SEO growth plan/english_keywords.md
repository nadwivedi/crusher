A query appearing in autocomplete means it has *some* recurring search demand; position in the list loosely indicates relative popularity. Cited as [Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=stone+crusher+software) (one representative URL; replace the `q=` value with the seed to reproduce).
- **No absolute search volumes were found.** Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Keyword Planner/Keyword Surfer data are login/extension-gated and were not accessible. Volume labels below (high/medium/low/very low) are **relative estimates** reasoned from: (a) whether the term autocompletes at all, (b) how early it autocompletes, (c) whether it spawns many sub-variants (city/state/unit modifiers = broad demand), (d) industry size (one Indian source cites ~12,000+ crushing units, so B2B software terms are inherently small) — [calcguru](https://calcguru.in/how-to-start-a-stone-crusher-business-in-india/) via search summary.
- Difficulty = who appears on page 1 in web search results (WebSearch tool, US-based index, so Indian SERP may differ somewhat).
- Rough volume ladder used: High = consumer/price/informational head terms with dozens of geo-variants (likely thousands+/mo in India); Medium = clear autocomplete with several variants (likely hundreds/mo); Low = autocompletes but few variants (likely tens to low hundreds/mo); Very low = does not autocomplete / long-tail (likely <10–30/mo). These bands are judgment, not measured.
