# Hindi / Hinglish search keywords for stone crusher software (crusherbook.com)

Research date: 2026-10-13. Method and its limits, stated once here so each claim below can be read correctly:

- **Google autocomplete** was queried directly (endpoint `suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=...`, India / Hindi locale). Google only shows a suggestion once a query has some minimum search volume. So "suggested" means *some real demand exists*, a long list of suggestions (state names, years, "today", "per ton" variants) means *strong* demand, and an empty list means *below Google's threshold* (weak or none). The threshold isn't published, so this ranks queries. It can't give monthly volumes.
- **YouTube autocomplete** (same endpoint, `ds=yt`) and **YouTube search result titles plus view counts** (youtube.com/results, India) were used as a second demand signal. They also show which words owners actually type.
- **Competition**: the general web-search tool available is US-centric and gave little for Hindi queries. Bing/DuckDuckGo scraping worked for only one query before being rate-limited, so Google SERP competition for Hindi queries is mostly **not directly verified**. Where a competitor is named, the evidence is a YouTube result, an autocomplete brand suggestion, or a vendor listing.
- No paid keyword-volume tool was available. Every "strong / medium / weak / none" rating below is relative and comes from the evidence cited next to it.

Autocomplete URL pattern used as the source in citations: `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=<query>` (abbreviated "[AC: query]"). YouTube autocomplete is "[YT-AC: query]" (`...&ds=yt&q=`). YouTube search results are "[YT: query]" (`https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<query>`).

## Which Hinglish and Devanagari queries show demand (autocomplete, related, YouTube)?

### Takeaway
Software-buying searches are typed in **Roman script, mostly English words plus "stone crusher"** ("stone crusher billing software", "crusher software free download"). Pure Hinglish software phrases ("crusher ka hisab", "crusher hisab kitab", "boulder ka hisab", "hyva bhada", "dumper ka hisab", "khata book crusher", "crusher munshi") return **no autocomplete at all**. Real Hinglish/Devanagari demand sits in adjacent jobs: **truck/gaadi ka hisab, truck bhada, kanta parchi, diesel ka hisab, JCB ka hisab, gitti ka hisab/rate/business, crusher business kaise kare / kharcha / profit**. Devanagari typing is common only for price and business questions (गिट्टी का रेट, गिट्टी का बिजनेस कैसे करें, कांटा पर्ची कैसे निकाले). Devanagari software queries (क्रशर सॉफ्टवेयर, क्रशर ऐप) get English suggestions back ("crusher software", "crusher app download").

### Inferences
- Owners searching for software **use English product nouns** ("software", "app", "billing", "accounting") even when the rest of the query is Hindi. Hinglish verb phrases ("hisab kaise rakhe") have demand only for generic objects (truck, gaadi, diesel, JCB, gitti), not for "crusher". A Hindi crusher page will get more traffic by targeting "truck/gaadi/diesel/kanta parchi ka hisab, crusher-specific" than by more variants of "crusher ka hisab".
- Devanagari is used for **price and business-idea** queries, not software. Pages should use a Hinglish slug and title with Devanagari body text and H2s (the pattern the existing two pages already follow).
- YouTube is the main competitor for Hindi informational queries (lakhs of views). Text pages are thin, which suggests a ranking opportunity for well-structured Hinglish pages. Google SERPs weren't directly verified, so treat this as likely, not proven.

### Gaps
- No absolute monthly search volumes (no Keyword Planner/Ahrefs access). Autocomplete presence only ranks queries.
- Google India SERP top-10s for the Hindi queries couldn't be retrieved reliably (the search tool is US-only, and Bing/DDG scraping was rate-limited or returned junk for Devanagari). Competition from Khatabook/Vyapar/OkCredit Hindi blogs is **unverified**. I found no evidence of them ranking for crusher-specific Hindi queries, and no evidence against.
- People Also Ask boxes couldn't be captured.
- Quora / Facebook group language for crusher owners wasn't reachable in the time available.

## Which keywords can share a page and which need separate pages?

### Takeaway
Group by intent, not by wording. Things that share one Google intent go on one page (business kaise kare + plant kaise lagaye; truck ka hisab + gadi ka hisab + dumper/hyva; kanta parchi app + dharam kanta parchi software). Keep separate pages where the intent differs (cost vs how-to vs profit; ledger app vs bhada calculator; gitti hisab vs gitti rate vs gitti business).

### Cited Findings
- Google autocomplete merges Hinglish and Devanagari inputs into the same completions ("क्रशर प्लांट कैसे लगाए" → "crusher plant kaise lagaye"; "कांटा पर्ची ऐप" → "kanta parchi app"; "क्रेशर प्लांट लगाने" → "crusher plant lagane ka kharcha").
