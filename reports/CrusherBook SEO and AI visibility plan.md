# Unblock the crawlers, then own crusher arithmetic

crusherbook.com cannot rank in Google or be recommended by AI assistants yet, and the cause is not missing keywords. Every one of its 35 URLs still sends crawlers the same 4,994-byte empty JavaScript shell, carrying the homepage title and a homepage canonical. Since the October 2026 move to react-helmet-async v3, the rendered pages also carry **two titles and two conflicting canonicals, with the homepage's listed first**. Google is therefore told that all 35 pages are copies of `/`, while ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot, whose crawlers do not run JavaScript, see no body text at all ([Google JS SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics); [Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler)). A search for `site:crusherbook.com` returned nothing, and the brand's Capterra listing outranks its own website ([Capterra IN](https://www.capterra.in/software/1241879/crusherbook)). The first fortnight should go to prerendering every route, cleaning `index.html`, adding a real 404 and redirects, and removing unsupported claims: a 4.8/250 rating nobody can see, "offline support", a "lifetime plan" and "royalty management". After that, the opportunity is unusually open. The English software keywords are small but weakly contested. Hindi crusher-software content barely exists anywhere. The big search demand sits in the daily arithmetic of the trade, which no crusher vendor serves: how many cft or tons fit in a hyva, brass-to-ton conversion, GSB/WMM/dust rates and HSN codes, JCB hour-meter and diesel maths, state mining-challan checks, and munshi register formats "in Excel with formula". AI visibility depends less on the site than on what others say about the brand. Brand mentions, review platforms, listicles and YouTube drive citations, and llms.txt shows no measurable effect ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/); [SE Ranking via SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/llms-txt-shows-no-clear-effect-on-ai-citations-based-on-300k-domains/561542/)). So reviews on Capterra and Techjockey, a corrected Techjockey listing, and Hindi YouTube carry as much weight as anything built on the site. One caveat covers the whole report: no paid keyword tool or Search Console access was available. Every demand figure is a ranking built from autocomplete and YouTube views, not a monthly search volume.

## Every crawler still sees one empty homepage, 35 times over

A live audit on 16 October 2026 caught a deploy landing at 17:33 GMT. After it, the live bundle (`index-CwbwtVPf.js`, 622 KB) matched the local build and **the sitemap matched the routes (35 = 35)**. Those are the only two problems from the 11 October audit that have been fixed. Every URL still returns HTTP 200 with the identical shell: real pages, trailing-slash variants, `www.`, `/index.html`, `/llms.txt` and `/this-page-does-not-exist-xyz` alike. That shell has no H1, no body text and no internal links. It does carry the homepage title and description, `canonical=https://crusherbook.com/`, and Organization and SoftwareApplication JSON-LD claiming an `aggregateRating` of 4.8 from 250 ratings ([live shell](https://crusherbook.com/stone-crusher-management-software); [sitemap](https://crusherbook.com/sitemap.xml)). GPTBot and ClaudeBot user agents receive the same file ([live fetch](https://crusherbook.com/crusher-billing-software)).

Rendering in headless Chrome showed a new problem. On React 19, react-helmet-async v3 "renders real DOM elements", and `<HelmetProvider>` becomes a passthrough. It no longer replaces the static tags in `index.html`; it adds a second set next to them ([react-helmet-async README](https://github.com/staylor/react-helmet-async)). So `/crusher-billing-software` renders with two `<title>` tags, two meta descriptions, two `og:url` tags, and **`canonical=/` followed by `canonical=/crusher-billing-software`**. The new 2,900-word buying guide renders the same way. Google's guidance covers this case directly. It says to set the canonical in HTML, to keep any JavaScript-set canonical identical to the original, and that "conflicting or multiple rel=canonical link tags may lead to unexpected results". It also says server rendering or prerendering "is still a great idea" because "not all bots can run JavaScript" ([Google JS SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics)). The likely result is that Google folds most of the 35 URLs into the homepage. That fits the evidence: in the test search engine, `site:crusherbook.com` returned no pages, and the brand query surfaced Capterra India and UK listings rather than the site ([Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1241879/crusherbook)). That search engine is not Google, so confirm in Search Console. The verification tag is already in `webpage/index.html`.

The quickest safe fix is a post-build snapshot, and it only works if `index.html` is cleaned first. Delete the static title, description, canonical, hreflang, all `og:`/`twitter:` tags and both JSON-LD blocks from `index.html`, so `Seo.jsx` becomes the only source of head tags. Then add a `postbuild` script that serves `dist/`, has Puppeteer visit each of the 35 routes, and writes `outerHTML` to `dist/<route>/index.html`. Generate `sitemap.xml` from the same route list so the two never drift apart again. Add a `NotFound` route with `noindex`, snapshot it to `dist/404.html`, and set `<html lang="hi">` on the four Hindi/Hinglish pages. Check that framer-motion's `initial={{opacity:0}}` does not freeze the H1 invisible in the snapshot. nginx then needs `try_files $uri $uri/index.html =404`, a 301 from `www` (including `http://www`) to the apex, a trailing-slash 301, gzip/brotli for `application/javascript` (CSS is already gzipped, so JS is simply missing from `gzip_types`), and `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` on `/assets/`.

The long-term path is React Router framework mode with `ssr: false` and `prerender`. This requires upgrading react-router from 7.13.2 to at least 7.18.4 for Vite 8 peer support ([React Router pre-rendering](https://reactrouter.com/how-to/pre-rendering)). `vite-react-ssg` is ruled out: it does not support React 19, and its own README points React Router 7 users to the built-in prerendering ([vite-react-ssg](https://github.com/Daydreamer-riri/vite-react-ssg)). Do the snapshot now, and move to framework mode when the blog and tools section is built. Field Core Web Vitals were not measured. Even so, a 622 KB uncompressed bundle that must run before anything paints is a real cost on rural 4G, and prerendered HTML removes that dependency.

The fix for cannibalisation is to choose one **owner page per cluster**. The other pages in a cluster get narrower modifiers, their own sections (screenshots, sample reports, Hindi terms) and cleaned `keywords`, H1 and title fields, or they get a 301. Google's spam policy describes "substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy" as doorway pages ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). The GEO study found keyword stuffing cut AI visibility by about 9% ([GEO paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3)). Both point the same way: fewer, deeper pages, linked from a `/solutions` hub in the navbar.

Trust fixes must ship alongside the rendering fix. Google allows `aggregateRating` only if "users should be able to see that aggregate rating on the page" ([Google review snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)). CrusherBook has zero ratings on Capterra and Techjockey, so the 4.8/250 rating must go until real reviews exist and are shown. The six named homepage testimonials (Raipur, Jabalpur, Rourkela, Dhanbad, Bilaspur, Katni) could not be verified: keep only real ones, with permission. Product claims contradict each other. `Features.jsx` sells "Royalty Management", "full offline support" and an "Offline Variant + Lifetime Plan", yet the raw-material page's FAQ answers "Does it track royalty?" with "No". An AI assistant that quotes the wrong one will mis-recommend the product. Finally, collapse the Organization schema into one node with `contactPoint`, one email (the schema says `support@crusherbook.com`, the footer `softwarebytesindia@gmail.com`), and a `sameAs` list of profiles confirmed to exist. Ship a real 1200×630 OG image with a correctly spelled filename, because every WhatsApp share currently previews the homepage.

## AI assistants recommend what others say, and CrusherBook has almost no off-site footprint

All five AI systems ground their answers in a search index, and none offers a special AI-only way in. Google says that to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode a page need only be indexed and snippet-eligible: "There are no additional requirements", and no special schema or AI files are needed. AI Overviews and AI Mode use "query fan-out", running related sub-searches, so their citations "can be broader and more varied" than classic results. `Google-Extended` does not control AI Overviews ([Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)). ChatGPT search depends on **OAI-SearchBot** being allowed; GPTBot is for training only ([OpenAI bots](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)). Perplexity depends on **PerplexityBot** ([Perplexity bots](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots)). Copilot draws on Bing. Bing Webmaster Tools added a free **AI Performance report** in February 2026, which shows per-page citation counts and the "grounding queries" behind them ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/bing-webmaster-tools-testing-new-ai-performance-report-468039)). How much ChatGPT relies on Bing is unsettled: one 2024 analysis found 87% overlap with Bing's top results ([Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)), while a later Ahrefs study of long-tail queries found about 8% ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/)). The safe reading is to be indexed well in both Google and Bing. CrusherBook's robots.txt (`User-agent: * / Allow: /`) already admits every bot ([robots.txt](https://crusherbook.com/robots.txt)), but those bots receive an empty shell. That is why prerendering comes first for AI visibility too.

Once a page is readable, the strongest correlate of being named in AI answers is **off-site brand presence**. Ahrefs' study of 75,000 brands found branded web mentions correlated at 0.664 with AI Overview mentions. Branded anchors came in at 0.527, branded search volume at 0.392, and backlinks at only 0.218 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)). Across 30 million US citations, Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn top the cited domains on every engine, with G2 sixth ([Peec AI](https://docs.peec.ai/research/most-cited-domains-in-ai-search)). One B2B study found 66% of ChatGPT brand recommendations came without citing the brand's own site ([Position Digital](https://www.position.digital/blog/chatgpt-ranking-factors/)). On the page itself, the only controlled evidence is the Princeton/IIT Delhi GEO paper (KDD 2024). Adding quotations raised visibility by about 43%, statistics by about 33%, and cited sources by about 28%. Lower-ranked sites gained most: citing sources lifted a rank-5 page by 115% ([GEO paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3)). That is a small new site's position. **llms.txt has shown no effect.** In SE Ranking's study of about 300,000 domains, having the file did not predict citations ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/llms-txt-shows-no-clear-effect-on-ai-citations-based-on-300k-domains/561542/)). It is cheap to add once pages are prerendered, but it is not a lever. Most of this evidence is correlational and vendor-produced, and none of it is India-specific.

What answer engines see today confirms the gap. A web search with an AI summary for "best stone crusher software India" drew on Capterra (CrusherBook, no ratings), Techjockey's LogiBit pages and "vs" comparisons, Quarry360's Issuu brochures and an IndiaMART seller. It said it found no independent ranking ([Techjockey](https://techjockey.com/detail/stonecrusher-software); [Issuu](https://issuu.com/quarry360/docs/the_fastest_stone_crusher_software_streamlining_y)). A Hindi query for crusher billing software returned no crusher-specific software at all ([LOGIC ERP](https://www.logicerp.com/blog/tag/invoicing-software/)). This was a proxy check; ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot themselves were not queried. Directories and marketplaces are therefore the surfaces that feed AI answers in this niche, and CrusherBook's are weak. **Techjockey still shows the old Rs 2,999/4,999/6,999 prices**, files it under generic "ERP Software" next to Odoo and Dynamics 365, and has no reviews ([Techjockey CrusherBook](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/crusherbook)). Capterra shows "Rated by 0 users". Every crusher competitor found has between 0 and 4 ratings, so **ten genuine reviews would lead the niche**.

## Four kinds of searcher, and only one is being served

The ~240-seed autocomplete sweep (Google India English and Hindi, YouTube, Bing India) shows that search behaviour divides into four groups, each with its own words. CrusherBook's sales pages speak only to the first.

The **owner or investor** shops for software in Roman script with English product nouns: "stone crusher billing software", "crusher software free download", "stone crusher accounting in tally erp 9" ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=crusher+software)). Even a Devanagari "क्रशर सॉफ्टवेयर" gets English completions back ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%89%E0%A4%AB%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B0)). Before buying a plant, the same person types Hinglish and Devanagari business queries. "stone crusher project report" fills ten slots with "pdf free download", "for bank loan", "in hindi" and "100/200 tph". Licence, subsidy, SBI loan, CPCB guidelines and "crusher kitne ka aata hai" follow ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=stone+crusher+project+report); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=crusher+kitne)). Road-material queries ("gsb rate", "gsb material", "wmm rate", "crusher dust", "m sand rate") each fill ten slots with per-ton/cft/brass, HSN and density modifiers ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=gsb+rate); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=crusher+dust)). The "hyva me kitna cft" argument is the same dispute that weighing on the kanta and billing per ton settles, and the product now records both cft and weight (commit 8e1ea36). That is the bridge from buyer search to the product.

Three typing rules follow for every Hindi page. The slug and the opening of the title are Hinglish in Roman script, and the H1, H2s and body are Devanagari with Roman keywords mixed in. Google merges the scripts: "कांटा पर्ची ऐप" completes to "kanta parchi app" ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9A%E0%A5%80%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%AA)). One page covers one intent in both scripts. Hinglish "hisab" demand exists only when the object is generic (truck, gaadi, diesel, JCB, tractor, gitti), never "crusher". "crusher ka hisab", "crusher munshi", "boulder ka hisab", "hyva bhada" and "crusher software in hindi" all return **empty autocomplete** ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=crusher%20ka%20hisab); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=crusher+software+in+hindi)). So the crusher angle goes inside generic-object pages ("crusher ke dumper ka hisab"). The existing Hinglish pages already cover the crusher-hisab phrasing, and more of it would be wasted.

Regional vocabulary decides the words used on state pages. **गिट्टी** is the word in MP, Chhattisgarh, UP, Bihar and Rajasthan. **रोड़ी** is used in Haryana/NCR, **stone chips** in Jharkhand, West Bengal and Odisha (Pakur chips), **khadi** (per brass) in Maharashtra, **kapchi** in Gujarat, and **jalli/blue metal** in Tamil Nadu ([Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/janjgir-champa-news/a-truck-s-ballast-costs-up-to-rs-5-000-now-people-are-finding-it-expe-8715971); [Amar Ujala](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/bhiwani/fall-in-the-prices-of-stone-dust-and-gravel-2023-02-04); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=hi&q=pakur+stone+chips); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=khadi+rate); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=en&q=jalli+rate)). **बजरी is a trap**: it means grit in Haryana but sand in Bhilwara and Uttarakhand ([Patrika Bhilwara](https://www.patrika.com/bhilwara-news/price-doubled-amid-strike-crusher-ballast-being-supplied-secretly-19849720)). Units vary just as much. A Chhattisgarh report treats "500 फीट" and "20 टन" as the same truckload, and everyday speech says "फीट" for cubic feet. State modifiers show up in three places: gitti price checks (UP, Bihar and MP cities), mining-challan "check/download by vehicle number" queries (Jharkhand, Bihar, UP, CG Khanij Online, MP eTP, Rajasthan e-rawanna, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Odisha i3ms), and licence or subsidy queries (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu). Nobody types "stone crusher software + state" ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=hi&q=jharkhand+mining+challan); [AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&gl=in&hl=hi&q=khanij+online)). Raipur and Bilaspur, the home market, have weak price-search demand.

## Conclusion

The research changes what "SEO for CrusherBook" means. The 29 keyword pages were built for a search engine that cannot read them and for software queries too small to carry the business. The two fixes that matter most work at opposite ends. One is a few days of engineering that makes the existing pages exist for crawlers at all. The other is slow trust-building off the site, in reviews, listings and video, which is where AI assistants form their view of a brand. They act on each other. Prerendering makes every review link, WhatsApp share and directory backlink point at a page with real content. Reviews and mentions in turn give AI systems a reason to name a brand they would otherwise skip.

The new keyword evidence also moves the strategic centre. The biggest unserved demand is not from owners shopping for software. It comes from buyers, munshis and operators doing the trade's arithmetic: cft in a hyva, brass to ton, hour-meter hire, diesel per hour, HSN codes, register formats. That arithmetic is exactly what the product's per-ton/per-cft billing, kanta-parchi capture and vehicle hire records settle. A small team that becomes the most quotable source on those numbers, in Hindi as well as English, gets cited by AI assistants as a side effect. The open risks are measurement (every volume here is a proxy) and accuracy: each tool and tax table must carry primary sources and dates, because a confidently wrong number gets quoted too.
