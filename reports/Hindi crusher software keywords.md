# Win Hindi search through trucks, kanta parchi and profit

Hindi-speaking crusher owners almost never search for "crusher ka hisab". They search for the jobs around the crusher: **truck/gaadi ka hisab, truck bhada, kanta parchi app, diesel ka hisab**, and, before and after they buy a plant, **crusher ka business kaise kare, plant lagane ka kharcha, and crusher business profit**. Google autocomplete returns nothing at all for "crusher ka hisab", "crusher hisab kitab", "boulder ka hisab", "hyva bhada", "dumper ka hisab" and "crusher munshi", so CrusherBook's two existing Hindi pages already cover the crusher-hisab phrasing and more variants of it would be wasted ([AC: crusher ka hisab](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=crusher%20ka%20hisab)). The supply side is just as thin. Apart from one Play Store app (Crusher Suvidha) and one Hindi demo video (Onedesk), no crusher software vendor publishes anything in Hindi, and Khatabook, Vyapar and the other big khata apps publish nothing in Hindi about crushers or gitti ([Crusher Suvidha](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.crushersuvidha.app)). The recommendation is **ten new Hinglish-slug pages with Devanagari body text**: three that sell (kanta parchi app, crusher gaadi/dumper hisab app, bhada hisab), four informational pages that carry a soft pitch (profit, plant kaise lagaye, plant ka kharcha, diesel ka hisab), and three narrower ones (gitti ka hisab calculator, Tally vs crusher app, gitti ka business). One limit runs through everything below: no paid keyword tool was available, so demand is ranked from autocomplete breadth and YouTube view counts, not from monthly volumes, and Google.co.in rankings were not directly observed.

## Owners type English product nouns and Hindi everything else

How people type matters more than any single keyword. When owners shop for software they use **Roman script and English product nouns**: "stone crusher billing software", "crusher software free download", "stone crusher accounting in tally erp 9" ([AC: crusher billing](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=crusher%20billing); [AC: stone crusher accounting](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=stone%20crusher%20accounting)). Typing "क्रशर सॉफ्टवेयर" in Devanagari still gets English suggestions back ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%89%E0%A4%AB%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B0)). Devanagari shows up for **prices and business ideas** (गिट्टी का रेट, गिट्टी का बिजनेस कैसे करें, क्रशर प्लांट कैसे लगाए). Google merges the two scripts into the same suggestions: "कांटा पर्ची ऐप" completes to "kanta parchi app" and "क्रशर प्लांट कैसे लगाए" to "crusher plant kaise lagaye" ([AC: कांटा पर्ची ऐप](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9A%E0%A5%80%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%AA)).

That gives every new page the same three rules. **The slug and the start of the title are Hinglish in Roman script.** The H1, H2s and body are Devanagari with Roman keywords mixed in, the pattern the two existing pages already use. **One page covers one intent in both scripts.** Never build separate Hindi and Hinglish pages for the same query.

The second rule is about *which object* goes in the keyword. Hinglish "hisab kaise rakhe" phrases have real demand only when the object is generic: truck, gaadi, diesel, JCB, gitti. They have none when the object is "crusher". "truck ka hisab" gives six suggestions, and YouTube adds "truck ka hisab kaise rakhe" and "truck hisab app" ([AC: truck ka hisab](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=truck%20ka%20hisab); [YT-AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&ds=yt&hl=hi&gl=in&q=truck%20ka%20hisab)). "diesel ka hisab" gives four suggestions on Google and five on YouTube ([AC](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&q=diesel%20ka%20hisab)). So the way into Hindi search is to **put the crusher angle inside pages that target the generic object**: "crusher ke dumper ka hisab", not "crusher ka hisab".

## Regional vocabulary decides which words go in the copy

Hindi news and seller listings show the core vocabulary shifts state by state, so pages need a "regional words" block rather than one standard Hindi term. **गिट्टी (gitti)** is the main word for crushed stone in MP, Chhattisgarh, UP, Bihar and Rajasthan ([Patrika Janjgir-Champa](https://www.patrika.com/janjgir-champa-news/a-truck-s-ballast-costs-up-to-rs-5-000-now-people-are-finding-it-expe-8715971); [Prabhat Khabar Nawada](https://www.prabhatkhabar.com/state/bihar/nawada/two-hiva-vehicles-loaded-with-ballast-seized)). **रोड़ी (rodi)** is the Haryana and NCR word, with "jeera rodi" for 10 mm ([Amar Ujala Bhiwani](https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/bhiwani/fall-in-the-prices-of-stone-dust-and-gravel-2023-02-04); [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/buildmakaan/aggregate-or-rodi.html)). **स्टोन चिप्स** is the Jharkhand word ([Prabhat Khabar Palamu](https://www.prabhatkhabar.com/state/jharkhand/palamu/illegal-stone-chips-sand-trucks-seized)). Fines are **डस्ट / स्टोन डस्ट** almost everywhere, and **धूला चूरा** in MP ([Zee News MP](https://zeenews.india.com/hindi/india/madhya-pradesh-chhattisgarh/mp/price-increased-of-stone-dust-grit-and-msand-for-house-construction-after-crusher-strike-ends-in-mp/2803428)).

The trap is **बजरी (bajri)**. It means crushed grit in Haryana and Gurugram ("jeera bajri") but **sand** in Bhilwara and Uttarakhand ([Patrika Bhilwara](https://www.patrika.com/bhilwara-news/price-doubled-amid-strike-crusher-ballast-being-supplied-secretly-19849720); [Janta Se Rishta](https://jantaserishta.com/local/uttarakhand/building-a-house-of-dreams-became-expensive-in-uttarakhand-the-price-of-sand-and-gravel-increased-know-the-new-rates-1469115)). Never use bajri alone for aggregate.

The vehicle split rests on a small sample of articles, so treat it as directional.

Three practical consequences follow. First, **units are a real pain point that CrusherBook already solves**: a Chhattisgarh report treats "500 फीट" and "20 टन" as the same truckload. That is the argument for weight-based billing from the kanta parchi, and it belongs on the kanta parchi, gitti hisab and profit pages. In copy, write "फीट" and "CFT", because everyday speech says "फीट" (even "वर्गफीट") for cubic feet. Second, **spelling variants** belong in body text and FAQs, never as separate pages. The observed ones are गिट्टी/gitti, क्रशर and क्रेशर (both appear, sometimes in the same outlet), hiva/hywa/hyva, rodi and rawanna/e-ravanna. Third, the transit-document words (रवन्ना, ई-टीपी, चालान, रॉयल्टी) are what the mining department enforces in every state sampled. CrusherBook does not handle royalty or permits, so these words may appear only in informational sections and never next to a feature claim.

One honest gap: the everyday staff and money words (munshi, khalasi, udhari, baki, khata, bhada at the crusher) were **not confirmed from any crusher-specific source**. Transport apps do use "truck khata", "driver khata", "bhada" and "bhatta" heavily on Play Store, which supports using them ([TransportBook](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.transportbook.app)). Words like kapchi, khanda and metal had no Hindi-belt evidence and are left out.

## Hindi competition is one app, transport apps and YouTube

**Crusher Suvidha** is the only Hindi-first crusher product. Its Play Store app defaults to Hindi with a Hinglish option, and it lists production logs, dispatch, stock, attendance, GST invoices and a **royalty register**, aimed at Tier 2/3 owners. It also works partly as a directory and news network ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.crushersuvidha.app)). No website or blog was found for it. If it starts publishing crawlable Hindi content, it becomes the main content competitor. **Onedesk** has a single Hindi demo video, "Stone crusher management software and mobile app Onedesk Demo (Hindi)" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QyyYRS0FgL0)). Every crusher software website checked (Crushsoft, Codeshilp, Quipu, Inniti, SAZS, OBAPS, LogiBit, Raseed) is **English only** ([Crushsoft](https://crushsoft.in/); [Quipu](https://quipu.in/stone-crusher-software/)).

The real Hinglish competition comes from neighbouring categories:

- **Transport apps** own the vehicle vocabulary. TransportBook sells "Truck Khata / Truck Hisab Kitab / Driver Khata" with trip-wise hire, advances and P&L ([TransportBook](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.transportbook.app)). TruckSarthi tracks bhada, diesel and bhatta per driver ([TruckSarthi](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sahilgumber7.trucksarthi&hl=en_US)). Both are built for transporters, not crusher owners who run their own and hired dumpers.
- **BharatFleet** owns the only free weighbridge slip tool, titled "Free Weighbridge Slip (Dharam Kanta) Generator". It does not use the words "kanta parchi", "bhada" or "hisab", and has no gitti or crusher fields ([BharatFleet](https://bharatfleet.com/weighbridge-slip-generator)).
- **Khatabook** runs a large Hindi "business kaise shuru kare" blog but has **no crusher or gitti post** ([Khatabook Hindi blog](https://khatabook.com/blog/hi/%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%A4-%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%82-%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%87-%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95-%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%98%E0%A5%81-%E0%A4%89%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%97-%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3-%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%BC%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B8/)). Vyapar's stone-crusher page is English ([Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/free/small-business-accounting-software/stone-crushers)). Khatabook could enter this topic at any time, which is a reason to move first.
- **Informational Hindi queries** are held by weak results:
  - TradeIndia Hindi price listings ([TradeIndia](https://www.tradeindia.com/hi/manufacturers/stone-crushing-plant.html))
  - 2020 Uttarakhand policy news ([ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/hindi/uttarakhand/state/dehradun/notification-of-stone-crusher-policy-with-doubled-license-fee-issued-by-the-government/uttarakhand20200105182913375))
  - YouTube videos with unverified income claims ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UMmKb76mAW0))
  - an English sales page under a Hindi URL path ([Project Report Bank](https://projectreportbank.com/hi/stone-crusher-project-report/))
  - an investkare Hindi post that now redirects to an unrelated domain

The gaps follow directly. No one owns these spaces:

1. A thorough Hindi guide to setup, cost and profit
2. Kanta parchi in a crusher context
3. Hyva or dumper bhada hisab for crusher fleets
4. Gitti business for traders
5. Play Store app names such as "Crusher Hisab", "Gitti Hisab" and "Kanta Parchi"

The last one is free: none of those titles is taken ([search results](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kaninfotech.mobile_weigh_bridge)).

Crusher Suvidha leads on royalty and GST, which CrusherBook should not try to match in copy. CrusherBook's own advantages are **vehicle hire by trip/ton/KM/monthly rent, AI reading of slip photos, diesel and spares issued to vehicles and the DG set, and munshi logins**. Every page below leads with one of these.

## Conclusion

The research changes where the Hindi opportunity sits. It is not a translated version of the English "crusher software" keyword set. It sits next door: **owners describe their problems through their trucks, their kanta parchi and their profit**, and no crusher vendor writes in that language. The three buying pages (kanta parchi, gaadi/dumper hisab, bhada) should go live first because they match features CrusherBook already has better than any transport app does. The profit and plant-setup pages are the long-term traffic engine, and they only win if they carry real, sourced numbers. The clickbait YouTube videos they compete against are beatable on trust, not on volume.

The same evidence points to two cheap moves beyond web pages. Putting "Crusher Hisab", "Gitti Hisab" and "Kanta Parchi" into the Play Store title and description claims names nobody holds. A short Hindi video showing a kanta parchi photo turning into a ledger entry faces only Onedesk's single demo. Both feed the pages above and cost far less than ten more articles.
